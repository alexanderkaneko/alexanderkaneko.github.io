About

Work in progress.

I spent this past summer doing research on diffusion models for ECG and PPG signals. In continuous space, steering a model toward what you want is fairly clean: you add a gradient to the score. When I started reading about diffusion language models I got stuck on the obvious problem. Tokens don't have gradients. There's no direction between two amino acids that means that its slightly more hydrophobic. So how do you steer one of these at all?

People have tried a few answers: relaxing tokens into something continuous, reweighting the categorical distribution, or treating generation as a search. I wanted to understand the tradeoffs myself, so I'm building a small framework that implements several of these on top of MDLM and pushes generation toward a target property, with a pretrained protein language model pulling back toward sequences that still look real.
